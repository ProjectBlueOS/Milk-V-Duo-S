Milk-V Duo S - SD Card Restore Instructions
=============================================

Image file: duo_s_backup.img.gz (374MB compressed)
Source: 29.1GB SD card, 3 partitions (boot 128M FAT32, raw 2M, rootfs 768M ext4)

RESTORE (Linux)
---------------
Identify your SD card device first! Check with: lsblk

gunzip -c duo_s_backup.img.gz | sudo dd of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress
sync

Replace /dev/sdX with your actual SD card device (e.g. /dev/sdb, /dev/sdc).
DO NOT use /dev/sda unless you are sure that is the SD card.

RESTORE (macOS)
---------------
diskutil list                  # find your SD card device
diskutil unmountDisk /dev/diskN

gunzip -c duo_s_backup.img.gz | sudo dd of=/dev/rdiskN bs=4m status=progress
sync
diskutil eject /dev/diskN

RESTORE (Windows)
-----------------
1. Install Etcher (https://etcher.balena.io/) or use Win32DiskImager
2. Decompress duo_s_backup.img.gz with 7-Zip to get duo_s_backup.img
3. Flash duo_s_backup.img to SD card using Etcher or Win32DiskImager

VERIFY AFTER FLASH
------------------
Insert SD card into Duo S, connect USB to Chromebook.

Chromebook terminal (Linux):
  sshpass -p "milkv" ssh root@192.168.42.1

Check process is running:
  ps | grep project_blue

Check logs:
  cat /root/project_blue.log

Browser client:
  Open PBControlStationV2.html
  Enter IP: 192.168.42.1
  Click Connect
