Project Blue - Milk-V Duo S SD Card Flashing Guide
================================================

You need: a microSD card (8 GB or larger) + card reader,
and the image file: duo-s-dist.img.xz (286 MB)

------------------------------------------------
WINDOWS (Balena Etcher - easiest, no extract needed)
------------------------------------------------
1. Install Balena Etcher: https://etcher.balena.io
2. Open Etcher -> "Flash from file" -> select duo-s-dist.img.xz
3. "Select target" -> your SD card (CHECK the drive letter!)
4. Click "Flash!" and wait for validation.
5. Eject, put the card in the Duo S (powered off), power on.

------------------------------------------------
WINDOWS (Raspberry Pi Imager)
------------------------------------------------
1. Install Raspberry Pi Imager: https://www.raspberrypi.com/software
2. "Choose OS" -> "Use custom" -> select duo-s-dist.img.xz
3. "Choose Storage" -> your SD card.
4. Click "Write". Eject, insert into Duo S, power on.

------------------------------------------------
LINUX (terminal)
------------------------------------------------
1. Find your card (CHECK carefully - wrong disk = data loss):
     lsblk
   (look for the ~size of your SD card, e.g. /dev/sdb)

2. Unmount it if auto-mounted:
     sudo umount /dev/sdX*

3. Decompress + write in one step (replace sdX):
     xz -dc duo-s-dist.img.xz | sudo dd of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress oflag=sync

4. Eject safely:
     sudo eject /dev/sdX

5. Put the card in the Duo S (powered off), power on.

------------------------------------------------
FIRST BOOT (all users)
------------------------------------------------
- The image is ~850 MB and fits any card 1 GB+.
- On first boot the system automatically expands to fill
  your whole SD card (takes about a minute). Just wait.
- Then connect: browser to http://192.168.42.1:8081
  (via the board's USB network), or as you normally do.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- Card not booting? Re-flash and let validation finish.
- "No space" errors? You flashed the .img, use a bigger card.
- Keep a backup of duo-s-dist.img.xz before writing.
